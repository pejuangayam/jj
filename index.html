Welcome to Sha PCB Homestay

Enjoy a relaxing and peaceful stay surrounded by comfort and nature. Our homestay offers a clean, cozy environment with modern facilities — perfect for families, friends, or weekend getaways.

Sha PCB Homestay Features

Sha PCB Homestay is fully equipped for a safe and comfortable family stay. The house includes CCTV security and free Wi-Fi for your peace of mind.

Living Areas

Two spacious sitting rooms with sofas and tables — one near the entrance and another in the middle section — complete with an Android TV for family entertainment.

Bedrooms

• 1 Master bedroom with queen bed, air conditioner, and ceiling fan
• 2 Queen bedrooms, each with air conditioner and fan (one includes a study desk)
• 1 Kids' bedroom with study desk, fan, and air conditioner

Dining & Kitchen Area

Equipped with Coway water purifier, refrigerator, air fryer, oven, rice cooker, and bread toaster — perfect for family meals. Includes washing machine and ironing board for your convenience.

Bathroom

Clean and well-maintained shared bathroom for guests' comfort.

Outdoor Area

Private swimming pool, BBQ space, and a dedicated prayer zone for guests.
